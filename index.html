Authors and provenance

Authors, translation and rights.

The mathematical works are by Emmy Noether and the co-authors named in them: Werner Schmeidler (Paper 17), R. Brauer (Paper 32), and R. Brauer and H. Hasse (Paper 38). The 1929/30 lectures were written up by M. Deuring; work 45 is by H. Kapferer, with a supplement written jointly with E. Noether.

Known differences

What still differs from the German text.

Some papers were translated from an earlier English translation whose formulas differ from the German text in places:

EditionPapers
Frenchparts of 24, 30, 31 and 34
Chinese, Simplified and Traditional characters24, 31, 32 and 34; parts of 35 and 40
Spanish23, 24, 30, 31, 32, 34 and 37; parts of 35 and 40 (in Paper 31, section 8.2 is shortened)
Russian, Ukrainian, Interslavic (both scripts)23, 24, 30, 31, 32, 34, 37 and 41–43; parts of 35 and 40 (Paper 43 is about a third of the full paper; Papers 41 and 42 are about four fifths)
Japanese32, 34 and 40; parts of 30, 35, 41 and 42 (Papers 40 and 41 are about three quarters)
  • Formulas: compared with the German source text, which follows Noether's printed pages, and brought into line with it: automatically where the comparison could be made, and by hand for the remaining formulas, including the layout of displays and tables and the letters that name the author's quantities. Some formulas still follow the earlier German transcription, mainly in passages that a translation renders in its own way; where the two differ, the German source text has the printed reading.
  • Paper 2: in the Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian and Interslavic editions, three footnotes of the German text (the note on Σ₃ and the citations of Gordan–Kerschensteiner, pp. 92 and 134) are marked only by an asterisk or attached to a neighbouring sentence; in the Japanese edition this applies to the note on Σ₃.
  • Paper 30: the notation may differ between editions in places.
  • Japanese: some readings in Papers 10, 13 and 38 may differ from the German text.
  • Spanish: some readings in Paper 20 and in the 1929/30 lectures may differ from the German text.
  • Chinese (both scripts): in Paper 38 a Fraktur 𝔭 may be printed as p.
  • The PDFs of the Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Interslavic and Japanese editions and of the German source text have no bookmarks.
